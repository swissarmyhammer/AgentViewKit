EK ^drp0v8k (on EK origin at 458b877a): `EditorDiff.FileDiff.Operation` covers every case of AVK's `DiffSummary.Operation`:
- `.added` → `.add`
- `.deleted` → `.delete`
- `.modified` → `.modify`
- `.renamed` → `.rename(from:)`
- `.copied` → `.copy(from:)`
- `.binary` → `.binary`
- `.unknown(String)` → `.unknown(String)`

`EditorSymbol.FileKind(diffOperation:)` maps it, with `.unknown` going to `FileKind.unknown`.

- Then delete `DiffSummary.Operation` and AVK's `EditorSymbol.FileKind(_: DiffSummary.Operation)` extension.
