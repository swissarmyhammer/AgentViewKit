title: Give attachment scopes the focus ring corners of their own surface
---
Found while working ^cwdb5zg. `AgentAttachmentScope` goes through `AgentItemScope`, which now chains `.scopeFocusRing(cornerRadius: theme.radii.l)` (the card radius). The attachment surfaces use other corners: `AttachmentChip` and `ArtifactView` draw `theme.radii.m`, and `AttachmentView`, `ImageView` and `AttachmentInspector` have their own shapes. When the EditorKit beacon of an attachment scope holds the focus (no focusable content of its own), its ring has the large card corners rather than the corners of the attachment surface.

- [ ] Check which attachment views let the beacon hold the focus (a chip that is a Button draws its own ring).
- [ ] For those, give `agentItemScope` / `agentAttachmentScope` the radius of the surface (for example a radius parameter that defaults to the card radius), so the ring follows the attachment's corners.
- [ ] Hosted test: the beacon's `drawFocusRingMask()` matches `RoundedRectangle(cornerRadius:)` of the surface radius (see `AgentItemFocusRingHostedTests.pixels(of:drawing:)`).
