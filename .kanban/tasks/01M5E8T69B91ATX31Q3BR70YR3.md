## Why

D113 answer 1: "The fills go. `agentContentSurface()` and the RequestDeck card fill (RequestDeck.swift:519) are both removed." On macOS the `.background` style is probably the window background color, an opaque fill. The demo puts `RequestDeck` in the composer slot (`Examples/AgentViewKitDemo/AgentViewKitDemoFeature/SessionThreadColumn.swift:29-33`), so this opaque card is in the bottom bar today (D112 (e): no opaque fill behind a bar).

## Files and lines

- `Sources/AgentViewKit/HumanInTheLoop/RequestDeck.swift:519` (`.agentContentSurface(.background)`) and `:520` (`.clipShape(AgentSurface.shape(for: theme))`).
- Tests of the deck in `Tests/AgentViewKitTests/HumanInTheLoop/`.

## What to do

- [ ] Replace `.agentContentSurface(.background)` with `.agentContentEdge()`.
- [ ] Keep `.clipShape(AgentSurface.shape(for: theme))` only if the content of the card must clip to the shape. If no content needs the clip, remove it, and say why in the commit.
- [ ] Add a hosted test with the ^dbbyg9w method (`ContentSurfaceHostedProbe`, or its new name from AVK-8w): show a deck with one request over the unique backdrop. A pixel inside the card frame has the backdrop color, with Reduce Transparency off and on.
- [ ] Risk: without a fill, the card text is over the scrolled list when the deck is in the bottom bar. The system scroll edge effect of the bar must keep it readable. Do not add a fill. Write a comment on AVK-8u (^8q34yt8) that asks the person to look at this card in the screenshots. If more is necessary, the answer is a `GroupBox`, not a fill (D113).
