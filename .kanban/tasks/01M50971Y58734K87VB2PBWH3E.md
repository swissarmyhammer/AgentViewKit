ScrollAnchorManagerTests covers what the manager does with a report, and SessionTranscriptViewHostedTests covers the scroll target id of each row. No test covers the link between them.

The demo app XCUITests (Examples/AgentViewKitDemo/Tests: ACPTabEndToEndTests, GalleryTabEndToEndTests) do not check this now.
