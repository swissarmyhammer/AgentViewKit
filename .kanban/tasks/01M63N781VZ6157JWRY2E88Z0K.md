EditorKit ^01kgwpk (EditorKit main 0372da7) made FocusRingBed public in EditorTestSupport. AVK's focus-ring tests mount their own bed. These files use AccentLook, RingColumn, `focus(_:in:system:by:)` or expectTheThemeAccent:
- Tests/AgentViewKitTests/Theme/SelectionSurfaceSitesHostedTests.swift
- Tests/AgentViewKitTests/Commands/AgentItemFocusRingHostedTests.swift
- Tests/AgentViewKitTests/Attachments/AttachmentFocusRingHostedTests.swift

API: `FocusRingBed(size:accent:appearsActive:system:content:)`, `capture()`, `ring(of:against:)` → `FocusRing` (`rows`, `pixels`, `isDrawn(in:)`), `FocusRingBed.referenceRing(size:cornerRadius:accent:)`, `defaultAccent` (.blue), `hostTint` (.red), `FocusRingBedError`.
