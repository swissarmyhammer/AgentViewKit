## Why

D113 answer 1 (testudoapp-4c) and TestudoApp `features/style.md` "How the steps apply": "Cards and panels in content have no fill. A thin `.separator` stroke in a `ConcentricRectangle` marks the edge. If the screenshot check shows that a group needs more, use a system container (`GroupBox`, or a `Form` with the grouped style), not a custom fill."

Today `agentContentSurface()` fills each content card with `.background.secondary` (`Sources/AgentViewKit/Theme/AgentSurfaces.swift:57-61`, `:99-100`). The fills go. This card adds the replacement modifier. The cards AVK-8w, AVK-8x, AVK-8y, AVK-8z and AVK-8aa move the callers. AVK-8ab removes `agentContentSurface`.

## Files

- `Sources/AgentViewKit/Theme/AgentSurfaces.swift`: add the modifier next to `AgentContentSurface` (do not remove `AgentContentSurface` in this card).
- `Tests/AgentViewKitTests/Theme/AgentSurfacesTests.swift`: add the tests.

## What to do

- [ ] Add `struct AgentContentEdge: ViewModifier` with `radius: CGFloat?`. Its body is `content.overlay(shape.stroke(.separator))`, where `shape` is `AgentSurface.shape(radius:)`, or `AgentSurface.shape(for: theme)` when `radius` is `nil`. It has no `.background` and no fill.
- [ ] Add `func agentContentEdge(radius: CGFloat? = nil) -> some View` in the `View` extension.
- [ ] Write DocC comments in STE. Name D113 and `style.md`. Say: no fill; if a group needs more, use `GroupBox` or a grouped `Form`, never a custom fill.

## Acceptance

- A test renders `Color.clear.agentContentEdge()` over a uniform backdrop of one unique color (`RenderedPixels`, light mode). The center pixel is the backdrop color. At least one pixel on the edge is not the backdrop color.
- A test checks that `AgentContentEdge(radius: r).shape(in:)` gives the path of `AgentSurface.shape(radius: r)` for each radius token, and the path of `AgentSurface.shape(for:)` for `nil` (copy the pattern of `theContentSurfaceOfARadiusUsesTheShapeOfThatRadius`).
