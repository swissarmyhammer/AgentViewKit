## Why

D110 (c): never glass on glass. The completion list and the no-commands note each have their own `.glassEffect`. They are an overlay on the editor, and the editor is inside the field glass of the composer. So their glass is on top of the field glass.

## Files

- `Sources/AgentViewKit/Input/EditorKitPromptEditor.swift`:
  - lines 100-107: the `.overlay(alignment: .topLeading)` that shows the list or the note.
  - lines 126-136: `completionList`, glass at line 135.
  - lines 140-149: `noCommandsNote`, glass at line 147.
- `Sources/AgentViewKit/Input/PromptInputView.swift` (the container of AVK-8i).

## What to do

- [x] Remove the two `.glassEffect` calls from `EditorKitPromptEditor.swift`.
- [x] Show the list and the note as a sibling glass shape in the composer `GlassEffectContainer`, outside the field glass. One method: the editor gives an anchor preference, and `PromptInputView` draws the list with `agentFloatingGlass()` at that anchor, above the field.
- [x] Keep the keyboard behavior: the editor keeps the focus, and the arrow keys, Return and Esc act on the list as today.
- [x] Keep the identifiers, for example `noCommandsIdentifier`.
