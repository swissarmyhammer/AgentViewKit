- [x] The line keeps its identifier `agent-status-line`, its label (the plain text of the line) and its value (the full path of the working folder).
- [x] The glass of `ThreadStatusHeader` is one container element with the identifier `agent-status-header` and the frame of the glass, inset included.

- [x] VoiceOver still reads the line with its label and value.
