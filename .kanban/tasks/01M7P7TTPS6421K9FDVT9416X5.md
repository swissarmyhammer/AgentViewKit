title: Bound the live EditorModels of a long transcript (EditorKit benchmark)
---
EditorKit benchmark (task 01M7NEQSSMHNCHQG87RM6WBVBS, macOS 27, release): 200 rendered messages take 3.47 s and 59 MiB (about 300 KiB each). A width change of 200 messages takes 115 ms, more than one frame. editorkit-7a advises: mount only the messages near the visible rows, and do not keep a live EditorModel and view for each message of a long transcript.

Now: ConversationView shows the rows in a LazyVStack (Thread/ConversationView.swift:299). Each ResponseView keeps its model in view state (OwnModelSlot), or in a CodeBlockModelCache when the environment has one (only the tests set one). A LazyVStack can keep the rows that scrolled away, so a long scroll can keep many models.

Do:
- Measure the live models after a scroll through a long recorded session (a unit or headless test that counts the models; no clock).
- If the count grows with the transcript, drop the model of a row far from the visible rows, or keep only the source text and make the model again on the next mount.
- Take the EditorKit follow-ups when they land: ^y1rptwk (mount cost) and ^2k996yf (height at a new width). editorkit-7a sends the commits.
